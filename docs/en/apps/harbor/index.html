Harbor · WebDAV / HTTP

A video player for your home NAS

Connect to WebDAV on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Browse your videos, build a playback queue, or download them for offline viewing.

Join the TestFlight beta↗Support

Beta version 0.0.1 supports individual video downloads; batch and folder downloads are planned for a later beta. The public test provides an iPhone / iPad build that also runs on compatible Apple silicon Macs.

From folders to offline playback

  • →Browse WebDAV folders, search the current directory, and check file types and sizes.
  • →Add videos or folders with subfolders to your queue, reorder them, and play continuously.
  • →Use native playback controls, full-screen previous and next, local history and resume.
  • →Download individual videos from your NAS, a direct link or the player; existing tasks are skipped.
  • →Track downloads, cancel or retry. Play completed files offline after restarting Harbor.

Your queue and local videos

Connections and history stay on your device

Harbor does not require a separate account and contains no ads or analytics SDK. Server passwords use the system Keychain. Playback and downloads connect directly to your server; TestFlight testing data is covered separately.
