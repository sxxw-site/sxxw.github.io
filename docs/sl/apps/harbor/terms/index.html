The app and your media

Harbor connects to WebDAV or HTTP / HTTPS video services you configure and plays or manages local copies. Apart from its clearly labelled original demo clips, Harbor does not supply a film catalogue, content subscription or rights to third-party videos.

Access, play and download only files you own or are authorised to use. Follow your server’s rules and applicable law. Rights to media remain with their respective owners.

Devices, connections and credentials

You are responsible for configuring a reachable server, maintaining your permissions, and securing your device and server credentials. Use trusted networks; HTTP is unencrypted. Harbor does not control server operators, home networks or system permissions.

You manage local queues, history and downloads. Deleting a local copy does not delete a server file; removing a server does not delete completed downloads. Keep important files backed up on the original server or another suitable location.

Beta status and feature limits

Harbor is currently in TestFlight testing. Eligibility, compatible devices, build expiry and testing-data processing are governed by TestFlight’s displayed information and Apple’s terms. Beta software can change, stop working or contain errors.

Playback depends on the installed build, system, device, codecs and files. Builds with VLC compatibility playback support MKV and other common containers; see Supported Formats in the app for the actual format and codec scope. Compatibility playback currently has no Teletext subtitles, Picture in Picture or AirPlay. SMB access, NAS transcoding and cross-device progress sync are not provided. Keep the app open for downloads; HLS / DASH offline downloads are unsupported. Compatibility with every NAS, file or network is not guaranteed.
