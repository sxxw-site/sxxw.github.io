Connect your home WebDAV server

  1. 01

    Enable WebDAV on your NAS

    Connect your device and NAS to the same reachable home network. On Synology, enable WebDAV Server in Package Center and give your account read access to the video folder.

  2. 02

    Add and test the connection

    Add a server in Harbor. Discover a local NAS or enter its WebDAV address manually, including the service port and shared folder. Enter your credentials, test and save. A DSM administration address is not a WebDAV address.

  3. 03

    Play or download your videos

    Open a video or add it to the queue from its menu. Choose Download to device from the video menu or player, then watch completed downloads in Local Videos. The Demo Library lets you try original silent clips before connecting a NAS.

Connections, playback and downloads

Why does discovery not find my NAS?

Allow local network access for Harbor. Check that both devices are on a reachable network and your NAS advertises compatible services with Bonjour. Guest Wi-Fi or access-point isolation may prevent connections. You can always enter an address manually. Finding a device does not sign you in.

Which WebDAV address should I enter?

Use the NAS WebDAV service address, port and shared folder. Synology defaults are port 5005 for HTTP and 5006 for HTTPS; use your actual server settings. HTTP is unencrypted and intended for trusted networks. HTTPS uses system certificate validation; invalid certificates cannot be bypassed.

Why will a particular video not play?

A container extension does not guarantee codec support. Playback depends on the installed build, system, device, encoding, embedded subtitles and audio tracks. Builds with VLC compatibility playback support MKV and other common containers, with system or compatibility engine selection where available; check Supported Formats in the app. Compatibility playback has no Picture in Picture, AirPlay or Teletext subtitles. SMB access and NAS transcoding are not provided. Try the bundled demo to check the player.

Where are downloads? Do they run in the background?

Open Local Videos to see progress, storage use and completed files. Keep Harbor open during transfers. Downloads are not guaranteed to continue when suspended or closed; unfinished tasks can be retried after reopening. Offline downloads support compatible standalone audio/video files, including MP4 and MKV, but not HLS / DASH playlists.

Why are existing downloads skipped?

Harbor skips an existing task for the same source, including completed, queued, active, failed or cancelled tasks. To transfer a failed or cancelled task again, choose Retry in Local Videos.

Can I download multiple videos or a whole folder?

The latest 0.0.1 builds let you select videos and folders, review the count and download in batches, including WebDAV subfolders. Existing tasks are skipped with a summary. Available features depend on the build you have installed.

How does deleting a download differ from removing a server?

Deleting a download removes its local copy, download record, and matching local queue and history entries. It leaves the NAS original untouched. Removing a server clears the connection, password, related online queue and history, and cancels unfinished transfers. Completed videos and download-source records remain until separately deleted in Local Videos.

How do I install the Mac app? How does it differ from TestFlight?

Download the Mac DMG from this website. It supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later. Open the disk image, drag Harbor to Applications, then open it. iPhone and iPad beta builds remain available through TestFlight, where eligibility, expiry and device requirements are shown.

Contact Harbor support

Include your app version, device, system version, steps to reproduce, and a redacted screenshot. Do not send NAS passwords, full token-bearing links or private videos. Email is sent only when you choose to send it.
