Start with your first entry

  1. 1

    Create your first entry

    Open the app and tap add, then fill in the name, date, and any notes you want to keep. Choose a birthday, anniversary, holiday, or custom event as needed.

  2. 2

    Choose the date rule

    Birthdays and anniversaries can use either the Gregorian or Chinese lunar calendar. Lunar entries automatically calculate the next matching date.

  3. 3

    Set reminders

    Turn on local notifications for important days and choose to be reminded on the day or in advance. If notification permission is off, the app will point you to system settings to enable it.

  4. 4

    Turn on sync when you need it

    To sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, turn on sync while signed in to iCloud. Sync goes into your private CloudKit database, which the developer cannot read.
