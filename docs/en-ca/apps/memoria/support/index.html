iOS · FAQ

Supports local reminders, widgets, Apple Watch, and optional iCloud private sync.

Get Started ↗
Will my data sync to other devices?

Once you sign in to iCloud and enable sync, your data syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch through your iCloud private library.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete records within the app; uninstalling the app removes local data, and iCloud data can be managed in the system's Settings → iCloud.

What if reminders don't appear?

Please make sure the entry has a reminder set and that the app is allowed to send notifications in system settings. Turning off notifications only affects reminders, not the records themselves.

HarmonyOS · FAQ

Fully local version: no network, no cloud sync; data stays only in the app's sandbox on the device.

Get Started ↗
Will my data sync to other devices?

No. This version doesn't connect to the network or offer cloud sync, so data is stored only on the current device.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete records within the app and empty the trash; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

What if reminders don't appear?

Please make sure the entry has a reminder set and that the app is allowed to send notifications in system settings. Turning off notifications only affects reminders, not the records themselves.
