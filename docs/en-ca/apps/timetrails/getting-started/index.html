足迹·TimeTrails Getting Started

Get started quickly, from granting location access to viewing your daily track.

OverviewGetting StartedPrivacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

Start with your first track

  1. 1

    Choose location permissions

    For continuous background recording, allow "Always" location and "Precise Location" when the system prompts you. You can change this anytime in system settings.

  2. 2

    Start recording

    Turn on recording the first time you open the app. You can always pause or resume within the app; you're in control.

  3. 3

    View your daily routes

    Look back on each day's route, mileage, stops, and activity summary in the calendar or on the map.

  4. 4

    Turn on backup when you need it

    If you want to restore when switching devices or sync across multiple ones, turn on iCloud data backup yourself in settings; it's off by default.
